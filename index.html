Rwanda Map Data

National-scale GIS outputs packaged as a KMZ for Google Earth Pro. All administrative layers, infrastructure, and survey results included.

Interactive Map (KMZ)

The KMZ file contains all the Rwanda-wide layers of the survey output. Download it and open in Google Earth Pro to view the map interactively.

⬇️ Download Map (KMZ)

How to open in Google Earth Pro

  1. Click the Download Map (KMZ) button above.
  2. Find map_compressed.kmz in your Downloads folder.
  3. Double-click the file — it opens automatically in Google Earth Pro.
  4. The map loads with all layers for Rwanda.
